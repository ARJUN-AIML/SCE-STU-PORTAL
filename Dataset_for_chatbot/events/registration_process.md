# Event Registration Process

## How do I register for an event?
To register for any college event or technical workshop:
1. Log in to the Student Portal.
2. Navigate to the **Events & Activities** tab.
3. Find the event you wish to participate in and click **Register**.
4. If the event requires an On-Duty (OD) letter, print the generated OD form.
5. Submit the OD form to your faculty advisor and HOD for approval at least 2 days before the event.
