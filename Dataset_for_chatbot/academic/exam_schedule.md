# Semester Examination Schedule

## Upcoming Semester Exams
The upcoming semester exams are scheduled to take place from **November 23, 2026 to December 15, 2026**.

### Guidelines
- Students must carry their original ID card and the printed Hall Ticket to the examination hall.
- Hall tickets will be available for download on the student portal one week before the commencement of exams.
- The Examination Cell (Exam Cell) is responsible for all examination schedules and grievances.
