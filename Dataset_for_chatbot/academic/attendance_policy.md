# College Attendance Policy

## Minimum Attendance Requirement
The minimum attendance requirement for all students across all courses is **75%**. Students with attendance below 75% will not be permitted to appear for the Semester End Examinations and will face a grade penalty.

## How is Attendance Calculated?
Attendance is calculated using the following formula:
`Attendance Percentage = (Total Number of Classes Attended / Total Number of Classes Held) * 100`

### Medical Exceptions
Students facing severe medical issues must submit a valid medical certificate to the HOD within 3 days of returning to college to get a maximum exemption of 10% (i.e., minimum requirement drops to 65%).
