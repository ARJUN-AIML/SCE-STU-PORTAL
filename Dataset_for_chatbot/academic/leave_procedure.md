# Student Leave Procedure

## How do I apply for leave?
To apply for a leave of absence, students must follow this official leave procedure:
1. Fill the leave application form (available at the department office or student portal).
2. Attach any necessary supporting documents (e.g., medical certificate for sick leave, OD letter for events).
3. Submit the completed application form to your faculty advisor for initial signature.
4. Final approval must be obtained from the Head of Department (HOD).
5. Approved leave letters must be submitted to the department office for attendance recording.
