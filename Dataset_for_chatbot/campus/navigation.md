# Campus Navigation Guide

## How do I reach Seminar Hall?
To reach the **Seminar Hall**, enter the main gate and walk straight towards the **RV Block**. The Seminar Hall is located on the **Ground Floor**, near the Main Entrance.

## Show the campus map
You can download or view the official campus map using this link: [Campus Map PDF](https://college.edu/campus-map.pdf). The map contains the layout of all academic blocks, hostels, and sports facilities.
