Q: What are the library timings?
A: The central library is open from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM on weekdays, and 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM on weekends.

Q: How can I apply for a hostel room?
A: Hostel room applications are available on the college portal under the 'Accommodation' tab. You must apply before August 1st.

Q: Who is the Head of the Computer Science Department?
A: The Head of the Computer Science Department is Dr. Alan Turing.
